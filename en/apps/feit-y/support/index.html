Getting in touch

Email pedrofeiteira.dev@gmail.com, or use the contact form. I read everything and reply within two working days. If you are reporting a problem, telling me your device, your iOS version and what you said to the assistant will get you a useful answer much faster.

Your account

You can sign in with an email and password, with Google or with Apple. To delete your account, open Account in the app and tap Delete account. That erases your account and everything in it — workouts, memory, food entries and progress photos — and cannot be undone.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple. To change or cancel one, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. A cancelled subscription stays active until the end of the period you have paid for. If you reinstall the app or move to a new iPhone, sign in to the same account and use Restore Purchases. Refunds are handled by Apple rather than by me, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Not medical advice

Feit-⁠Y records your training and answers questions about it. It is training guidance, not medical advice: if something hurts, or you have a condition that affects how you train, talk to a health professional.
